My First Year at CSUSM

Jordan Kopp

A New Beginning

Starting the first year at California State University San Marcos is both exciting and overwhelming. From navigating the sprawling campus to meeting new classmates, students quickly learn how to balance independence with responsibility. Orientation week sets the tone, introducing Cougars to the supportive community and academic expectations that will define their college experience.

Finding Balance

As the semester unfolds, first-year students discover the importance of time management. Balancing lectures, assignments, and social life becomes an art form. Many take advantage of campus resources like the library, tutoring center, and student organizations to stay on track and connected. It’s a year of trial, error, and personal growth.

Building Community

By spring, what once felt unfamiliar starts to feel like home. Friendships deepen, routines solidify, and students begin to envision their paths at CSUSM. Whether through study groups, campus events, or late-night conversations in the dorms, the first year lays the foundation for both academic success and lasting memories.
